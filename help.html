About the hub

Ram-Gear Shop Apps is a launcher for Ram-Gear Manufacturing's shop web apps. It is one page with a tile for each app; tap or click a tile to open that app in a new tab.

Hub address: https://ram-gear.github.io/app-hub/

The apps

QC Form

Gearbox assembly verification and teardown evaluation forms. Works offline and syncs when connected. Needs the QC sign-in.
https://ram-gear.github.io/ramgear-app/

Web Gear Calc

Install the hub

Android phone

  1. Open https://ram-gear.github.io/app-hub/ in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then confirm.

"RG Shop Apps" appears on your home screen and opens full-screen.

Ask Grok about this ↗

iPhone

  1. Open https://ram-gear.github.io/app-hub/ in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Ask Grok about this ↗

PC

  • In Chrome or Edge, open https://ram-gear.github.io/app-hub/ and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  • Or double-click the local copy: Z:\pricebook\PROGRAMS\Ram-Aps\index.html
